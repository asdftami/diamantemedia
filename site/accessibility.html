Accessibility statement

LAST UPDATED: NOVEMBER 7, 2026

Accessibility comes first in everything I design, this site included. It's built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. If anything gets in your way, tell me and I'll fix it.

What's built in

  • Keyboard: every link, button and form field works with Tab, Enter and Space, with a bright yellow focus outline. A "Skip" link on each page jumps straight to the content.
  • Screen readers: pages use real headings, landmarks and labels. Images have descriptions, decorative graphics are hidden, and status messages (like form errors) are announced.
  • Color contrast: text meets or beats the AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1, and most text is far higher.
  • Motion: an Animation switch on every page stops the starfield, spinning planets and floating stickers, and the site follows your device's "reduce motion" setting automatically.
  • Sound: nothing plays on its own. The contact phone's key beeps only play when you tap a key, and Key sounds turns them off.
  • Touch: buttons and links have tap areas of at least 44 × 44 pixels.
  • Forms: every field has a visible label, and errors explain how to fix them, right next to the field.
  • Images: case study images open larger with one click or Enter, and you can browse them with the arrow keys and close with Esc.
  • Zoom and phones: the layout reflows for small screens and browser zoom, with nothing cut off.
  • Predictable navigation: the menu is the same on every page, and every page opens at the top.

Known limitations

  • The phone keypad on the contact section is a playful extra for mouse and touch. Keyboard and screen reader users can type in the Message box as usual.
  • Some case study images are screenshots or slides with small text. Each has a caption with the key point, and you can enlarge any image.
  • The custom retro cursor appears only with a mouse. Text fields keep the standard text cursor.

How it was tested

Keyboard-only navigation, color contrast checks, reduced-motion and Animation-off modes, and phone and desktop screen sizes. Screen reader testing is ongoing.

Report a problem

If something on this site is hard to use, or you need any content in another format, please send me a message and tell me the page and what happened. I'll get back to you and fix it.
